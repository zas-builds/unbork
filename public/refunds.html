Refunds and cancellations

Last updated 20 November 2026

Ask within seven days of paying and you get all of your money back, without having to explain yourself.

Paid reports are not on sale yet. This page sets out the policy in advance, so it is public before anyone can be charged.

What you are buying

One full report for one website address: the fix steps and a paste-ready prompt for each problem found, plus seven days of free re-checks of that same address. It is a single payment of ₹799 in India, or $9 if you are paying from outside India. It is not a subscription, so nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel later.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email [contact email, to be added] within seven days of paying.
  2. Include the payment reference Razorpay sent you, or the address you checked.
  3. You do not have to give a reason. We will not ask you for one, and we will not try to talk you out of it.

We aim to start the refund within two working days of your email. Razorpay returns the money to whatever you paid with; how long it then takes to appear is up to your bank or card issuer.

If something goes wrong at our end

If a payment went through but you never got your report, that is a refund without any seven-day limit. Email us with the payment reference and we will either deliver the report or return the money, whichever you prefer.

What we will not refund

Nothing, inside seven days. After seven days, a report that was delivered and worked is not refundable, because what you bought was the information and you have it. If you believe something is genuinely wrong with the report itself, write to us anyway and say so.

Cancellations

There is no recurring payment, no stored card and no account to close. If you simply want us to forget you, there is nothing to forget: paying for a report stores a one-way fingerprint of the address, the payment reference and the date your free re-checks end, and no copy of your report. The privacy policy has the detail.
