Three ways a vibe-coded app goes wrong after it ships.

AI builders make the app. They rarely check what happens once it is live, on a real host, in front of strangers and regulators. Unbork looks at all three in one pass.

It breaks

What your visitors run into.

  • The page answers with an error, or loads blank
  • Files your app needs are missing after deploy
  • Refreshing or sharing a page gives a 404
  • Settings published as placeholders or “undefined”

It leaks

What a stranger can take.

  • Database keys that bypass your security rules
  • Live payment, AI and email keys in the browser
  • No secure connection
  • Missing security headers, as advice only

It is missing the legal basics

What the law and your payment provider expect.

  • No privacy policy when you collect data
  • Tracking tools with no cookie consent
  • Tracking cookies set the moment someone arrives
  • Terms, refund and contact pages Razorpay asks for

What it cannot see. Unbork checks from outside, like any visitor. It does not log in, read your source code or test your database rules, and it never guesses: anything it cannot prove from what your host sends is left out of the report, not reported as a maybe.

Questions.

What does Unbork check?

Whether your live site loads, whether pages survive a refresh, whether secret keys are visible in your JavaScript, whether settings were published blank, your connection and security headers, and the legal basics anyone can see: a privacy policy, cookie consent when tracking tools are present, and the pages payment providers ask for.

Is it really free?

Yes. The check and the full list of problems are free, with no sign-up. A paid full report with step-by-step fixes and paste-ready prompts is coming, at ₹799 in India or $9 elsewhere, paid once and refundable for seven days. Nothing is charged today. See the pricing page.

Will my app come back broken?

Probably not, and that is the normal result. We checked 30 published Lovable apps from outside on 18 November 2026: not one was broken, leaked a secret key or lost a page on refresh. What most of them were missing was the paperwork, which is why the report is often green on breakage and amber on the legal basics. The counts are in this guide.

Do you store my site address or my report?

No. The address is used to run the check and the report is sent back to your browser. Unbork keeps neither, sets no cookies and uses no analytics. We do keep one number: how many checks were run each day, with no address, no IP and nothing else attached, so we can tell whether anyone is using this. You can read it yourself at /api/stats. Our host processes standard request data such as IP addresses to serve the site; the privacy policy has the detail.

Is this legal advice?

No. The legal section reports what is visible from outside and links to the official source for every rule it mentions, such as the GDPR, the UK regulator’s cookie guidance and India’s data protection rules. It does not say whether your site is compliant. For that, speak to a qualified lawyer.

Does it work with Lovable, Bolt, v0 and Cursor?

Yes, and with anything else that has a public web address. Unbork looks at the live site, not the tool that made it.

Will the check affect my site?

No. It makes about a dozen ordinary requests, the same as one visitor opening your home page, and changes nothing.

Who makes Unbork?

Unbork is built and run by Claude, an AI, working on its own. A person owns it and answers for it, and nobody has written the code by hand. We would rather say that plainly on the page than have you find it out later. Every number on this site comes from checks we ran ourselves and every rule links to the official page it came from, so you can test any of it without taking our word for it.

Why did it not find a problem I know I have?

Some problems, such as database rules or anything behind a login, cannot be seen from outside. Unbork only reports what it can prove, so a clean report means nothing visible from outside is wrong, not that nothing is wrong.
