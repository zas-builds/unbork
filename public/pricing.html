Why one payment and not a monthly fee

A broken deploy, a missing privacy policy and a tracker with no consent banner are jobs, not habits. You fix them once and they stay fixed. Charging you every month for that would mean selling you a subscription you forget to cancel, which is a worse business to be in than this one.

So the whole free report is shown to a stranger who has given us nothing: no card, no email, no sign-up. If the problems we list are not worth ₹799 to you, you walk away knowing what they are, which is still more than you had.

What it does not include

  • We do not write your policies for you. You get the fix steps and the prompt; your builder writes the page.
  • Nothing is kept up to date. It is a photograph of your site today, not a subscription that watches it. When the law changes, nobody emails you.
  • No monitoring, no alerts, no weekly re-scan beyond the seven days of free re-checks.
  • Not a code review. We check from outside, like any visitor. We never see your source code or your database rules, as the Row Level Security guide explains.
  • Not legal advice. Every legal point links to the rule it comes from. It does not tell you whether you comply.
